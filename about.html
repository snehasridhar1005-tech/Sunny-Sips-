Our Story

Hello from the
bright side.

A youth-oriented fruit beverage startup born in Bengaluru to turn ordinary drink breaks into joyful, vibrant moments.

🎓 CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru

Meet the Founders ☀️

Vaishnavi Somasundaram & Sneha Sridhar

We are two aspiring entrepreneurs and BBA students from CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, with a shared passion for business, creativity, and entrepreneurship.

As young individuals ourselves, we understand the need for refreshing, affordable, and exciting beverage options. This inspired us to create ON THE BRIGHT SIDE (Sunny Sips), a youth-oriented brand that aims to make everyday drink breaks more enjoyable through fun flavours, fresh drinks, and a vibrant experience.

Our goal is to build a brand that brings people together, one refreshing sip at a time!

“A Brighter Kind of Refreshment.”
